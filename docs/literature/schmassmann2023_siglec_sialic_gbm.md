| IDs | DOI 10.1126/scitranslmed.adf5302 · PMID 37467314 |
| Data | Human survival association (SIGLEC9); mouse microglia- and monocyte-specific Siglec-E KO + scRNA-seq |

## Facts reported (abstract)
- High Siglec-9 expression correlates with reduced survival in GBM patients.
- In mice, Siglec-E inhibits phagocytosis by microglia and monocyte-derived cells; losing it on monocyte-derived cells improves cross-presentation and T-cell priming.

## Relevance
- Together with Mei 2023, Siglec-9 is the best-studied myeloid lectin in GBM. Useful as a **positive control** in our sc data (SIGLEC9 should mark monocyte-derived TAMs).
- The mouse findings are not human data (constraint: human only).
