## Facts reported
- Classes: **CLEC12A (MICL)** and CLEC4A (DCIR) are ITIM inhibitory C-type lectin receptors (SHP-1/2). **MGL (CLEC10A)** and DC-SIGN are "non-canonical, signalling-modulatory": GalNAc ligands lower DC glycolysis and favour IL-10 programmes.
- MGL avidity rises with the number and density of GalNAc residues on mucin glycopeptides.
- It cites Pace 2026 for ASGR2/CLEC12A as GBM network hubs. No single-cell data on ASGR2 are cited.

## Relevance
- An independent 2026 review confirms that ASGR2/CLEC12A in GBM rest on Pace 2026 alone, so the sc question is still open.
- The ITIM vs modulatory split is a framing for the Discussion, not a test.
