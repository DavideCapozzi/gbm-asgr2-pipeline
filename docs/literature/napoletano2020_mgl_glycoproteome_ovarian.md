## Facts reported
- MGL (on DCs) recognises the tumour Tn antigen. The MGL binders found sit on the membrane, inside the cell and in the matrisome, so MGL may sense microenvironmental cues.

## Relevance
- This is the lab's ligand-side method. For storyline (b) it gives a **list of carrier proteins** whose genes could be checked in GBM tumour cells. Limit: the list comes from ovarian cancer and is protein/glycan level, while our data are mRNA.
