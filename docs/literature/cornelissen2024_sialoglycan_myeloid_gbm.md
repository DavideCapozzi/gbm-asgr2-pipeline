## Facts reported
- GBM cells induce CD163 on co-cultured monocytes; removing sialic acid from the GBM cells reduces this induction and raises IL-6/TNFα.
- Triggering Siglec-7 or -9 with antibodies lowers TNFα, which supports a Siglec–sialic acid axis.
- No hub C-type lectins in the text.
