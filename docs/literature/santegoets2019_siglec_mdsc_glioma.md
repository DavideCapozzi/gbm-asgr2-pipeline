## Facts reported
- M-MDSC and PMN-MDSC both express Siglec-3, -5, -7 and -9; Siglec-3/-7/-9 higher on M-MDSC, Siglec-5 higher on PMN-MDSC; similar profile in healthy donors.
- Siglec-5/-9 confirmed on PMN-MDSC in glioma tissue.
- Freshly isolated glioma cells carry mainly **sialic-acid ligands for Siglec-7 and -9** (also in situ).
- No C-type lectin hubs (ASGR2, CLEC12A, CLEC10A) in the text.

## What our sc data can test
- Siglec expression per myeloid state at mRNA level (SIGLEC7/9 vs ASGR2/CLEC12A in the same cells). This is the Siglec counterpart of storyline (a).
