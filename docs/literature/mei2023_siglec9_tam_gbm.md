## Facts reported (abstract)
- Monocyte-derived TAM subsets with high **SIGLEC9** accumulate in anti-PD-1 non-responders.
- Deleting Siglece (the mouse homolog) restrains tumours, activates CD4/CD8 T cells and synergises with anti-PD-1/PD-L1.

## Relevance
- A **Siglec at single-cell level in human GBM is already published** (high impact). Storyline (a) must not rest on Siglecs; its novelty is in the C-type hubs (ASGR2, CLEC10A).
